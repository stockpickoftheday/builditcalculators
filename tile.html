How the maths works

One tile's area, then divide

A 12 by 12 inch tile covers 1 square foot. A 12 by 24 inch tile covers 2. Divide your floor area by one tile's area and you have the tile count before waste. The calculator does the inches-to-feet conversion for you — just type the tile size in inches.

Always add waste

Tiles get cut at the edges, around door frames and around pipes, and a few always break. Ten percent is the standard allowance for straight rows. Go to 15% for diagonal patterns, herringbone, or rooms with lots of corners and angles.

Buy a spare box

Tile dye lots change between batches, and a box from a different lot can look slightly off. Buying one spare box now — same lot, same color — is far easier than hunting for a match in two years when one tile cracks.
