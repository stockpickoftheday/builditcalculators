How much flooring do I need?

A 12 by 15 foot room takes 198 square feet of flooring with 10% waste — 10 boxes of laminate at 20 square feet a box, or 9 boxes of vinyl plank at 24. Measure the room, add waste, and divide by what is in a box.

How the maths works

Step 1: the room area

Length × width. A 12 by 15 foot room is 180 square feet. Measure each room separately — flooring is ordered by the room, not the house.

Step 2: add waste

Add 10% for a straight lay in a rectangular room, 15% for a diagonal layout or a room with lots of corners, and 20% for herringbone. The 12 by 15 room needs 198 square feet with 10% waste.

Step 3: divide by the box size

Laminate is usually about 20 sq ft a box, vinyl plank about 24, hardwood about 20 and engineered hardwood about 22. The exact number is printed on the box. 198 ÷ 20 = 9.9, so 10 boxes of laminate; 198 ÷ 24 = 8.25, so 9 boxes of vinyl plank.

Underlayment

Allow one roll of underlayment per 100 square feet — 2 rolls for the 12 by 15 room.

Common questions

How much flooring do I need for a 12×12 room?

144 square feet, and 159 with 10% waste — 8 boxes of laminate at 20 square feet a box.

How much extra flooring should I buy?

10% for a straight lay in a rectangular room. Use 15% for diagonal layouts or rooms with lots of corners, and 20% for herringbone.

How many square feet are in a box of flooring?

It varies by brand — laminate is usually about 20, vinyl plank about 24, hardwood about 20, and engineered hardwood about 22. The exact number is printed on the box.

How much underlayment do I need?

About one roll per 100 square feet — 2 rolls for a 12 by 15 foot room.
