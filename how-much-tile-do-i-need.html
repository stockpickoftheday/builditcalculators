How the maths works

Step 1: how many tiles

Divide the floor area by one tile's area. A 12×12 inch tile covers 1 square foot, so a 120 square foot room takes 120 tiles. A 12×24 inch tile covers 2 square feet, so the same room takes 60.

Step 2: add waste

Add 10% for straight rows, 15% for diagonal patterns, herringbone, or rooms with lots of corners. The 10 by 12 room needs 132 twelve-inch tiles with 10% waste.

Step 3: divide by the box

Divide by the tiles per box and round up. 132 tiles in boxes of 10 is 13.2 — buy 14 boxes.
