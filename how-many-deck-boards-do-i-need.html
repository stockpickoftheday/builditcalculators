How-to guide

How many deck boards do I need?

A 12 by 16 foot deck takes about 410 linear feet of 5.5 inch decking — roughly 2.13 linear feet of board for every square foot of deck. Add joists at 16 inches on center and about 350 screws per 100 square feet, and you have the whole shopping list.

How the maths works

Decking boards: 2.13 linear feet per square foot

A 5.5 inch board with a ⅛ inch gap covers 5.625 inches, so one square foot of deck takes 2.13 linear feet of board. Multiply your deck area by 2.13 to get the linear feet, then round up — and buy a couple of spare boards for cuts, because a board cut wrong is the most common waste on a deck.

Joists: 16 inches on center

Joists run under the decking, 16 inches apart. Divide the deck's length by 16 inches and add one joist for the end. A 12 by 16 foot deck has 13 joists.

Screws: two per board at every joist

Count about 350 screws per 100 square feet of deck — two screws where each board crosses each joist. A 12 by 16 foot deck takes 672 screws.

What size boards?

5.5 inch wide boards (sold as 1×6) are the standard for decking. Wider boards cover faster but cup more in the sun; narrower boards look busier and use more screws. The deck calculator works with 5.5 inch boards.

Common questions

How many deck boards do I need for a deck?

It depends on board width. A 5.5 inch board with a ⅛ inch gap covers 5.625 inches, so one square foot of deck takes 2.13 linear feet of board.

How far apart should deck joists be?

16 inches on center, which is the standard for 5/4 decking.

How many deck screws do I need?

About 350 screws per 100 square feet of deck — two per board at every joist.

How much decking do I need for a 10×10 deck?

213 linear feet of 5.5 inch decking.
