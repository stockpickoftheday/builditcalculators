Privacy Policy

We do not collect your measurements, your name, or anything else about you. The calculators run entirely in your browser — nothing you type leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, and no form that sends us your numbers. The calculators are plain JavaScript that runs on your own device.

Cookies and advertising

We plan to show ads through Google AdSense in the future, once the site is approved. When that happens, Google may use cookies to show ads — including advertising cookies that remember which sites you visited. You can turn off personalized advertising in your Google ad settings at any time. We do not sell personal information, because we do not collect any.

Other services

This site loads no external fonts, libraries, or tracking scripts. The only files it loads are its own pages, one stylesheet and one script.
