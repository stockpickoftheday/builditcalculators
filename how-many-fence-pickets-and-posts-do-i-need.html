How many fence pickets and posts do I need?

100 feet of 6 foot privacy fence takes 14 posts, 241 pickets and 39 rails — plus 14 bags of concrete, one per post. The trick is the pickets: a 1×6 picket is really only 5.5 inches wide, so it takes 2.2 pickets per foot, not 2.

How the maths works

Posts: length ÷ spacing, plus one

Divide the fence length by the post spacing — usually 8 feet — then add one post at the end. 100 feet ÷ 8 = 12.5, so 13 sections and 14 posts.

Pickets: 2.2 per foot

1×6 pickets are really 5.5 inches wide. That is 2.2 pickets per foot of fence — 219 pickets for 100 feet, 241 with 10% waste for cuts and the odd bad board.

Rails: 3 per section

A 6 foot privacy fence needs three horizontal rails per section — top, middle and bottom. 13 sections × 3 rails = 39 rails.

Concrete: one bag per post

One 80 lb bag sets a 4×4 post in a 10 to 12 inch hole, 24 inches deep. 14 posts means 14 bags. The concrete calculator works out post holes on their own.

How deep should the posts go?

At least a third of the post goes underground, and below your frost line — 24 inches deep is the usual minimum for a 6 foot fence. Go deeper in cold climates, where the frost line is lower.
