About Home & Garden Calculators

Eight free calculators for American homeowners — concrete, paint, mulch, topsoil and gravel, tile, drywall, fence and deck. Type in your measurements, get the answer in seconds, and see the maths so you can check it yourself.

What this site is

Every calculator on this site answers one question: how much material do I need? We use US units — feet, inches, gallons, cubic yards, pounds — and plain English, no jargon. Every formula is shown on the page, with a worked example using real numbers, so you can follow the maths yourself.

What it is not

It is not professional advice. The numbers are estimates for planning: good enough to order materials and budget a job, not a substitute for a contractor, an engineer, or your local building code. Prices and local rules change — confirm them with your supplier before you buy.

How the numbers are worked out

Every formula on this site was checked against published sources in November 2026 — manufacturer data sheets (Quikrete, USG), university extension guides (Texas A&M AgriLife), the US Army's earthmoving manual, and several independent calculators. Where sources disagree, the page says so and uses the safe planning number.

What is coming next

We plan to add free printable project checklists you can download and take to the store — a shopping list for each calculator, so nothing gets forgotten. The calculators come first; the downloads will follow.

This site is educational only. It is not financial advice and not professional construction advice.
